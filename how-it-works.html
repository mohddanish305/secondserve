Report Surplus Food
Logistics & Quality Chain

From Excess to Nourishment in 3 Hours

Food rescue is an urgent race against the clock. Here is our step-by-step cold-chain verified rescue protocol.

1 ⏱️ Step Time: < 15 Mins

1. Quick Surplus Notification

When a banquet, corporate cafeteria, or wedding reception finishes, the organizer submits our simple 60-second online form or dials our helpline. No registration or app download needed. Our operations desk immediately registers the pickup in our database with a unique tracking ID.

2 ⏱️ Dispatch Time: 60–90 Mins

2. Food Safety Check & Collection

Certified volunteer Food Heroes arrive with food-grade stainless steel containers and digital infrared thermometers. Food must register above 65°C for hot items or below 5°C for chilled items. We check sensory smell, texture, and preparation time before sealing containers.

3 ⏱️ Delivery Time: Same Evening

3. Delivery & Dignified Service

Our fleet transports the sealed containers directly to vetted beneficiary sites: government night shelters (Rain Baseras), slum community kitchens, or child welfare shelters. Meals are served warm with dignity, ensuring zero good food is lost to municipal landfills.

🛡️

Strict Hygiene & Food Safety Protocol

Every pickup is temperature-checked and packed hygienically. We follow FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) surplus food regulations without compromise:

  • Fresh & Untouched: We only collect untouched buffet surplus and kitchen batch food that has never been served onto individual diner plates.
  • Temperature Integrity: Hot food must be received above 65°C and served within 4 hours; chilled items below 5°C.
  • Segregated & Labelled: Strict segregation of vegetarian and non-vegetarian containers with tamper-evident seals.
  • Traceability: Every batch logs donor venue, volunteer ID, pickup time, and destination shelter for radical accountability.
