Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries from food donors, CSR partners, and night shelters.

Food pickup is 100% free of charge for donors. Our electric vehicles, food-grade storage containers, driver stipends, and testing equipment are financed entirely through transparent CSR grants and philanthropic donors.

We accept freshly cooked, untouched batch meals from banquets, hotel buffets, corporate dining halls, and catered events. We accept both vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes (which are segregated into dedicated containers). We do not accept half-eaten plates or food stored without temperature control.

In our core city service zones, our volunteer vans arrive within 60 to 90 minutes of receiving your report. For late-night wedding receptions (11 PM – 2 AM), our on-call Event Food Heroes dispatch immediately upon phone confirmation.

Our minimum pickup threshold is food for at least 10 people to ensure vehicle route efficiency. For smaller household quantities, we encourage dropping off packaged food directly at our nearest community kitchen drop-point or partnering with your residential society for joint pickups.

All financial contributions qualify for 50% tax exemption under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act (Order: AAKCS1234EF20261). Upon receiving your financial contribution, our automated system generates an official stamped 80G receipt with your PAN details.

Yes. Registered shelter coordinators can contact us using the form above or call our helpline to be added to our distribution roster. We match your shelter's evening headcount with nearby regular banquet routes.

Second Serve rescues surplus food from weddings, hotels, and banquets to serve hot wholesome meals to night shelters across India.
