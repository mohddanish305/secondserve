Our Mission & Ethics

About Second Serve

Bridging the divide between India’s wedding banquet surplus and urban community hunger with dignity, transparency, and cold-chain speed.

Founded in 2021, East Delhi

Born from a Simple, Heartbreaking Contrast

On a chilly November night in 2021 in East Delhi, our founder attended a cousin's wedding where over 200 portions of untouched paneer dishes, dal makhani, and freshly made naan were being carried to commercial dumpsters. Just 800 metres away beneath the flyover, families in an unorganised settlement were going to bed on empty stomachs.

The food was delicious, freshly prepared, and completely wholesome. The only missing element was a reliable, temperature-safe bridge between the two. That night, two volunteers packed the food into steel pots and delivered it directly to a local shelter. Within 24 hours, Second Serve was born.

“India doesn't suffer from a lack of food; it suffers from a lack of rapid, compassionate redistribution.”

Our Core Convictions

  • ✨
    Dignified Nourishment: Food rescue is not charity scraps; it is distributing fresh, high-grade banquet food with unconditional respect.
  • ⏱️
    Speed Over Bureaucracy: Donors report in 60 seconds; our vans dispatch within 90 minutes.
  • 🔍
    Radical Openness: Every meal counter connects to real database deliveries. We welcome scrutiny from every donor and volunteer.
Milestones

Our Journey of Impact

2021

The First 1,000 Meals

Founded in Laxmi Nagar, Delhi. Personal cars and borrowed stainless steel milk cans deployed for night rescues.

2023

Multi-City Expansion

Launched city chapters in Mumbai and Bengaluru, partnering with 75 hotel banquets and university volunteers.

2024

Cold-Chain Fleet

Introduced custom electric vans equipped with temperature-logging sensors and food-grade insulated storage crates.

2026

3.2 Lakh Meals

Operating across 6 cities with 850 volunteers, real-time database tracking, and radical cost transparency.
